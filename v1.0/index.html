Standard Model Epistemic Graph

State / model / observable concept graph of particle physics, grown step by step: one-particle quantum mechanics → Wigner's representations → quantum fields on Fock space → effective interactions → electroweak gauge theory and the Higgs sector. Scope: QED and the electroweak sector (strong sector omitted).
